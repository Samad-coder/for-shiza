My Shiza,

I can't undo those moments with a letter, and I don't want to hide behind excuses or ask you to forget what happened. I understand that trust comes from what I consistently do, not from what I promise when I'm afraid of losing you.

Your feelings matter. Your dignity matters. You should never have to choose between being loved and being respected.

If you ever feel ready to talk, I will listen—properly, patiently, and without trying to argue you out of how you feel. Until then, I will give you the space you've asked for.
